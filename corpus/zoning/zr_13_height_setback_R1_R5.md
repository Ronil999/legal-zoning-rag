# NYC Zoning Resolution — Article II, Chapter 3
## Section 23-42: Height and Setback Requirements in R1 Through R5 Districts
**Source:** NYC Department of City Planning — zr.planning.nyc.gov  
**Last Amended:** 12/5/2024  
**Retrieved:** September 2026

---

### 23-420: General Applicability

In the districts indicated, the height and setback regulations of a building or other structure shall be as set forth in Section 23-421 (Basic pitched-roof envelopes for certain districts) and Section 23-422 (Basic flat-roof envelopes for certain districts). Where applicable, standard setback provisions are set forth in Section 23-423 (Standard setback regulations).

Such heights may be increased on **qualifying residential sites** or on zoning lots containing **qualifying senior housing**, or for certain **large sites**, pursuant to Section 23-424 or Section 23-425, respectively. Additional provisions are set forth in Section 23-426 and Section 23-44.

The height of all buildings or other structures shall be measured from the **base plane**.

---

### 23-421: Basic Pitched-Roof Envelopes for Certain Districts

**Applicable districts:** R1, R2, R3A, R3X, R3-1, R3-2, R4, R4-1, R4A, R5A — for single- or two-family detached, semi-detached, or zero lot line buildings or other structures, where permitted.

**Perimeter wall height:** Perimeter walls are subject to setback regulations at a maximum height above the base plane of 25 feet.

**Ridge line:** Sloping planes control the maximum height of the building. These planes start at the maximum permitted height of the perimeter walls (25 feet) and meet at a ridge line of 35 feet above the base plane.

**Apex point:** Each perimeter wall with a horizontal dimension of 8 feet or more which projects from an adjacent perimeter wall at least 18 inches may have an apex point directly above it on the 35-foot-high plane, provided a line drawn from the intersection of two perimeter walls to such apex point does not exceed 80 degrees to the horizontal.

---

### 23-422: Basic Flat-Roof Envelopes for Certain Districts

**Applicable districts:** R3-2, R4, R4B, R5, R5B, R5D — for buildings not using the pitched-roof envelope of Section 23-421.

| District | Maximum Base Height | Maximum Building Height |
|---|---|---|
| R3-2 | — | 35 feet |
| R4 | — | 35 feet |
| R4B | — | 25 feet |
| R5 | 35 feet | 45 feet |
| R5B | — | 35 feet |
| R5D | — | 45 feet |

For **R5** (no letter suffix), at a height not higher than the 35-foot maximum base height, a setback shall be provided in accordance with Section 23-423 (Standard setback regulations) before continuing to the 45-foot maximum building height.

**Note:** R3-2 and R4 buildings using a flat roof are capped at 35 feet regardless of the perimeter-wall/ridge-line envelope of Section 23-421, which only applies to pitched-roof buildings in those districts.

# NYC Zoning Resolution — Article II, Chapter 3
## Section 23-361: Maximum Lot Coverage in R1 Through R5 Districts
**Source:** NYC Department of City Planning — zr.planning.nyc.gov  
**Last Amended:** 12/5/2024  
**Retrieved:** September 2026

---

**Single- or two-family residences:** For zoning lots with buildings containing single- or two-family residences, the maximum residential **lot coverage** shall be:

| Lot Type | R1, R2 | R3 | R4, R5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Interior or through lot | 40% | 50% | 60% |
| Corner lot | 80% | 80% | 80% |

**Exception:** In R2X, R3A, and R3X Districts, the maximum residential lot coverage shall be whatever lot coverage remains after the required yards set forth in Section 23-32 (Front Yard Requirements) and Section 23-34 (Rear Yard and Rear Yard Equivalent Requirements) are applied — there is no separate percentage cap in those specific districts.

**Multiple dwelling residences:** For standard zoning lots developed with multiple dwelling residences, maximum lot coverage is 80% for interior or through lots and 100% for corner lots.

**Large sites:** Where the height and setback modifications for large sites under Section 23-425 apply, the overall maximum lot coverage across the site is 50%, though individual portions of the site may exceed 50% provided they do not surpass the applicable non-large-site maximum for their lot category.

**Applicability note:** R3-2 falls under the "R3" column (50% interior/through, 80% corner). R4 falls under the "R4, R5" column (60% interior/through, 80% corner).

# NYC Zoning Resolution — Article III, Chapter 3
## Section 33-122: Maximum Floor Area Ratio for Commercial Buildings in Commercial Districts
**Source:** NYC Department of City Planning — zr.planning.nyc.gov  
**Last Amended:** 12/5/2024  
**Retrieved:** September 2026

---

For a zoning lot containing only **commercial uses** in a Commercial District whose bulk is not governed by the surrounding Residence District regulations (see Section 33-121), the maximum **floor area ratio (FAR)** shall not exceed the amount set forth in the following table.

| Districts | Maximum Commercial FAR |
|---|---|
| C3 | 0.50 |
| C4-1, C8-1 | 1.00 |
| C1-6, C1-7, C1-8, C1-9, C2-6, C2-7, C2-8, C7-1, C8-2, C8-3 | 2.00 |
| C4-2A, C4-3A, C7-2 | 3.00 |
| C4-2, C4-2F, C4-3, C4-4, C4-4D, C4-5, C4-6, C4-8, C4-9, C4-11, C4-12 | 3.40 |
| C4-4A, C4-4L, C4-5A, C4-5X, C5-1, C7-3 | 4.00 |
| C4-5D | 4.20 |
| C7-4, C8-4 | 5.00 |
| C6-1, C6-2, C6-3 | 6.00 |
| C7-5 | 6.50 |
| C7-6 | 8.00 |
| C6-3D | 9.00 |
| C4-7, C5-2, C5-4, C6-4, C6-5, C6-8, C7-7 | 10.00 |
| C6-11, C7-8 | 12.00 |
| C5-3, C5-5, C6-6, C6-7, C6-9, C6-12, C7-9 | 15.00 |

**Community facility floor area:** Where a zoning lot contains community facility uses, or both commercial and community facility uses, the applicable maximum FAR is instead governed by Section 33-123, not this Section.

**Districts governed by residence bulk rules:** C1 and C2 districts mapped within a Residence District (and certain C4 districts specified in Section 33-121) follow the FAR of the underlying Residence District rather than this table.

**Applicability note:** C4-2, the commercial district referenced elsewhere in this corpus's site records, has a maximum commercial FAR of 3.40 under this table.

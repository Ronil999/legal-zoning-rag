# NYC Zoning Resolution — Article II, Chapter 3
## Section 23-21: Floor Area Regulations for R1 Through R5 Districts
**Source:** NYC Department of City Planning — zr.planning.nyc.gov  
**Last Amended:** 12/5/2024  
**Retrieved:** September 2026

---

In all R1 through R5 Districts, **floor area** shall be calculated as set forth in Section 12-10 (DEFINITIONS) and shall not exceed the maximum **floor area ratio (FAR)** specified in the following table.

| District | Maximum FAR (Standard Zoning Lots) | Maximum FAR (Qualifying Residential Sites) |
|---|---|---|
| R1-1, R1-2, R1-2A | 0.75 | 1.00 |
| R2, R2A | 0.75 | 1.00 |
| R2X | 1.00 | 1.00 |
| R3, R3A, R3X, R3-1, R3-2 | 0.75 | 1.00 |
| R4, R4-1, R4A, R4B | 1.00 | 1.50 |
| R5, R5A, R5B | 1.50 | 2.00 |
| R5D | 2.00 | 2.00 |

**Single dwelling unit cap:** For standard zoning lots with a lot area of 4,000 square feet or more, the maximum residential floor area associated with any single dwelling unit shall not exceed an equivalent floor area ratio of 0.60, regardless of the district's overall maximum FAR.

**Qualifying residential sites:** The higher FAR column applies only to zoning lots meeting the definition of a **qualifying residential site** under Section 12-10 (DEFINITIONS) — generally sites developed with multiple detached or semi-detached buildings under common ownership. Standard interior, corner, and through lots use the standard-lot column.

**Note on district suffix variants:** R3-2 and R4 are the district designations referenced elsewhere in this corpus's site records; both fall under the standard 0.75 and 1.00 FAR rows respectively, not the higher R4A/R4B contextual rows.
